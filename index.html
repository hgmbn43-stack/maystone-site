How it works

Four steps. You decide at the end.

  1. A quick chat

    Two minutes on the phone about your work, and what people find when they look you up.

  2. Your free preview

    Say yes, and we build a preview website from your Google reviews and business details. We send the link by text, WhatsApp or email – your choice.

  3. Have a look

    Open it on your phone and tell us what you'd change. It's not on Google, and we don't share the link with anyone.

  4. Your call

    Like it? Pick an option below and pay on Stripe's secure page. Not for you? We take it down. No chasing.

Privacy

Your details, in plain English.

Who's responsible

Harvey-George Matthews, trading as Maystone. Contact: hello@maystone.uk.

What we use

  • Business details that are public on Google: your business name, trade, area, business phone number, rating and reviews.
  • What you give us: your email or mobile, logo, photos, and notes from our calls.
  • Call recordings, when a call is recorded.

Why we use it

  • To tell local businesses about a service that could help them, and to build your preview if you say yes (legitimate interests).
  • To build and run your website if you buy (our contract with you).
  • To keep accounts records (the law requires it).

Who sees it

The Maystone team, and the services we use to run the business: Google Workspace (email and files), GitHub (hosting previews and websites) and Stripe (payments). We never sell your details.

How long we keep it

  • Call recordings: up to 30 days, only for training. Object, and we delete yours the same day.
  • Sales notes: up to 12 months after we last spoke, then deleted.
  • Customers: while we look after your site, plus 6 years for accounts records.
  • Do-not-call list: your number stays on it, so nobody here rings you again.

Your rights

Ask for a copy of your details, ask us to correct or delete them, or tell us to stop contacting you: email hello@maystone.uk. We act within 7 days, and always within one month. Unhappy with how we've handled it? You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk or 0303 123 1113.

Sales calls

You can stop sales calls from most businesses by registering your number free with the Telephone Preference Service, or the Corporate TPS for companies: tpsonline.org.uk.
