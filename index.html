Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Reps never pay to join, train or level up, and never buy stock. Our partners pay us; you never do.

Is this an MLM or pyramid scheme?

No. You earn only from real sales to real customers. You never pay us, never buy stock, and nobody earns from simply recruiting people.

Do I need sales experience?

No. Free training, scripts and practice calls are included before you start.

How and when do I get paid?

By bank transfer, for every sale that's cleared the customer's 14-day refund window, with a statement each time. Payday is set out in your rep agreement. You work as a self-employed contractor, so you handle your own tax.

Can I really work any hours?

Yes. There are no set shifts or minimum hours. Calls to businesses happen 8am to 7pm, Monday to Saturday.

Who can join?

Anyone in the UK aged 18 or over who's registered as self-employed with HMRC. Students, side hustlers, career changers and experienced closers are all welcome.

Who do reps sell for right now?

HGM Leads, our sister web studio: websites for local tradespeople. We'll add more partners as the team grows.

Privacy

Your details, in plain English.

Who's responsible

Harvey-George Matthews, trading as Maystone Sales Group. Contact: hello@maystone.uk.

What we use

  • Businesses we call: details that are public on Google (business name, trade, area, business phone number, rating and reviews), plus what you give us and notes from our calls.
  • People who apply to join: what you send us.
  • Call recordings, when a call is recorded.

Why we use it

  • To tell local businesses about a service that could help them, and to build your preview if you say yes (legitimate interests).
  • To build and run your website if you buy (our contract with you).
  • To consider your application if you want to join.
  • To keep accounts records (the law requires it).

Who sees it

The Maystone team, HGM Leads (to build previews and websites), and the services we use to run the business: Google Workspace (email and files), GitHub (hosting websites and previews) and Stripe (payments). We never sell your details.

How long we keep it

  • Call recordings: up to 30 days, only for training. Object, and we delete yours the same day.
  • Sales notes: up to 12 months after we last spoke, then deleted.
  • Applications: up to 12 months if you don't join.
  • Customers: while we look after your site, plus 6 years for accounts records.
  • Do-not-call list: your number stays on it, so nobody here rings you again.

Your rights

Ask for a copy of your details, ask us to correct or delete them, or tell us to stop contacting you: email hello@maystone.uk. We act within 7 days, and always within one month. Unhappy with how we've handled it? You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk or 0303 123 1113. You can also register free with the Telephone Preference Service to stop most sales calls: tpsonline.org.uk.
