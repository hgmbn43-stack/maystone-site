Your path

From first call to your own team.

This is how reps climb at Maystone. Your results decide how fast you go.

  1. Day 1

    Apply in 2 minutes

    We call you for a chat. If it's a fit, you sign your rep agreement and get your own login.

  2. Week 1

    Train, then call

    Free scripts, objection handling and practice calls. Then your first real calls, on your hours.

  3. First sale

    Paid, and on the board

    Commission paid once the customer's 14-day refund window clears. You're on the leaderboard.

  4. Level 2

    Top Rep

    Stay in the top 10% and get first pick of new roles from new partners.

  5. Level 3

    Coach

    Train new reps and earn a bonus on their sales.

  6. Level 4

    Team Lead

    Run a team across a role or region. Self-employed, your team, your business.
